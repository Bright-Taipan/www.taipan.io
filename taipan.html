Why Taipan

Fast where it counts, careful where it matters

Six traits that cut across every module — some you can already feel in the demo above.

01

Massive efficiency

Simple questions get simple, fast paths through the model. Taipan doesn't spend a heavyweight reasoning pass on a lookup a calculator could answer.

02

Relevant reasoning

Reasoning is aimed at your actual question, not every tangent that's technically true. Depth goes where it changes the answer.

03

Multilingual by default

Kiswahili, English, French, Arabic, Spanish and more — switchable mid-conversation without losing the thread.

04

Loaded settings

Reply depth, tone, and language are settings you control for the session — not fixed behaviour baked into every answer.

Coding powerhouse

Multi-file projects, multiple languages, real compilation and tests — see the full pipeline in the Workshop module below.

Photo featuring

The stack

Eight modules, one Taipan

Modules 01–04 give Taipan a mind: something to think with, remember with, sense with, and speak with. Modules 05–08 give it hands — ways to reach outside the conversation and get real work done.

Live today In progress Concept
A — the mind
 
01

Brain

Started from an open-weight language model, then shaped for Taipan's own voice and standards — not trained from a blank page.

  • Conversation, reasoning, and step-by-step problem solving
  • Writing, editing, and plain-language explanations
  • Programming, mathematics, and science
  • Translation and summarisation
  • Long, complex instructions held across a long conversation
02

Memory

Say "I'm learning C++" today, and Taipan still knows it next week when you ask it to teach you functions.

  • Short-term: this conversation, recent messages, the current task
  • Long-term: preferences, learning goals, decisions you've made
  • Project memory: code, documents, and past versions of a project
  • You hold the pen — view, edit, or delete anything Taipan remembers
B — the senses and hands
 
03

Senses

A student photographs a physics question and asks "explain this step by step" — Taipan reads the image and teaches from it.

  • Images, PDFs, tables, and screenshots
  • Code, in any file, in context
  • Audio and speech transcription
  • Video
04

Voice

"Taipan, explain Newton's laws" — spoken, answered naturally, no typing required.

  • Speech-to-text and text-to-speech
  • Full voice conversations, low-latency
  • Multiple voices and speaking styles
  • Natural interruptions, mid-sentence
Module 05

Toolbelt — Taipan knows when it doesn't know

Taipan doesn't pretend to have today's news memorised. It routes each request to the right tool, on its own — that's what makes it agentic rather than just a chat window.

Module 06

Ask for a project and Taipan doesn't just paste code into the chat. It builds, compiles, tests, and fixes it — then hands back something that actually runs. Multi-file projects, most major languages, one continuous session from first line to last fix.

Module 07

Classroom — six ways to learn, not one

A tutor who always gives the answer isn't teaching. Taipan switches modes depending on whether you need an explanation, a nudge, or a test.

Teacher mode

Explains a concept clearly, from the ground up.

Tutor mode

Gives hints instead of handing over the answer.

Exam mode

Generates practice questions at the right level.

Revision mode

Turns a topic into tight, usable revision notes.

Quiz mode

Tests you directly and marks the result.

Step-by-step mode

Breaks a hard problem into manageable pieces.

🇹🇿 Taipan TIE Mode

Built around the Tanzanian curriculum

A dedicated mode with knowledge organised the way Tanzanian students actually encounter it — aligned to the TIE syllabus, in the subjects and sequence students are taught in, from form one onward.

Module 08

Studio — image generation

The last piece of the stack: turning a description into a picture, a diagram, or a first draft of a design.

Still on the workbench. When it ships, Studio will sit alongside the Workshop — sketch a UI, illustrate a concept for Classroom mode, or generate a diagram straight out of a conversation.
